# Government Scheme — Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) Cashless Hospitalization

## Category: Government Welfare Schemes & Health Rights
## Citation: National Health Authority (NHA) — PM-JAY Guidelines
## Statutory Urgency: High (During medical emergencies / hospitalization)

### Coverage Scope & Entitlements
Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) is the world's largest public health assurance scheme, offering:
- **₹5,00,000 per family per year** for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization across public and empaneled private hospitals.
- Cashless and paperless access to healthcare services at the point of care.
- Covers pre-existing conditions from Day 1; no waiting period, no family cap on size or age.
- Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses including medicines and diagnostics.

### How to Check Eligibility and Avail Treatment:
1. **Eligibility Verification**: Visit https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in or call toll-free helpline **14555**. Verified using Ration Card, SECC database, or Aadhaar.
2. **Ayushman Card Generation**: Can be downloaded via the Ayushman App or created at any empaneled hospital helpdesk (PMAM - Pradhan Mantri Arogya Mitra desk).
3. **Empaneled Hospitals (EHCP)**: Check whether the treating hospital is listed on https://hospitals.pmjay.gov.in.
4. **Zero Out-of-Pocket Expense**: Empaneled hospitals cannot charge beneficiaries for bed charges, surgery, ICU, consumables, or implants covered under the pre-defined HBP (Health Benefit Package).
5. **Grievance Redressal**: If an empaneled hospital demands cash or denies admission to an eligible cardholder, register immediate grievance at https://cgrms.pmjay.gov.in or call **14555**.
