# Municipal Property Tax Notice & Disputed Assessment Challan

## Category: Civic Notices & Municipal Compliance
## Citation: State Municipal Corporation Act & Self-Assessment System (SAS)
## Statutory Urgency: Medium (Early payment rebate vs penalty interest)

### Understanding Property Tax Assessment Notices
Municipal corporations (such as BBMP, BMC, MCD, GCC, GHMC) issue annual property tax challans or re-assessment notices when:
- Annual Property Tax has become due for the fiscal year.
- A physical or GIS survey indicates an undeclared floor addition, alteration in built-up area, or change of property usage (e.g. residential converted to commercial).
- Discrepancy detected between Self-Assessment Scheme (SAS) declarations and municipal master records.

### Key Deadlines & Financial Incentives:
1. **Early Bird Rebate**: Most corporations offer a **5% rebate** on property tax if paid in full within the first month of the financial year (typically April 1 to April 30 / May 31).
2. **Penalty for Delayed Payment**: Outstanding dues past the deadline attract interest of **1.5% to 2% per month** simple interest until paid.
3. **Disputed Demand / Escaped Assessment**: If a penalty notice demands arrears for past years due to alleged area discrepancies:
   - Check the exact Survey Number, Assessment Number (PID / Khata / Property ID), and calculation sheet attached.
   - You have **30 to 45 days** to submit an objection before the Assistant Revenue Officer (ARO) or Municipal Assessor.
   - Do not pay a contested penalty without obtaining an official acknowledgment of your written objection.

### Step-by-Step Payment & Verification:
- Pay only via official municipal e-governance portals (e.g. BBMP Property Tax, MCD Online, Aapli Chikitsa/BMC) or authorized bank counters.
- Never hand cash to informal agents without an instant computer-generated official municipal receipt.
- Verify receipt displays the updated transaction reference and zero outstanding arrears balance.
