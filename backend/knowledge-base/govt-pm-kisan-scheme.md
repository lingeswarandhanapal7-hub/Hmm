# Government Scheme — PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi Rules & e-KYC Verification

## Category: Government Welfare Schemes
## Citation: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi Operational Guidelines
## Statutory Urgency: Medium (Installment eligibility cycles)

### Scheme Overview & Financial Benefits
The PM-Kisan scheme provides income support of **₹6,000 per year** to all landholding farmer families across India, payable in three equal four-monthly installments of **₹2,000 each** (April-July, August-November, December-March), transferred directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.

### Mandatory Compliance Requirements for Installment Release:
1. **Mandatory e-KYC**: Every beneficiary farmer must complete e-KYC on the PM-Kisan portal (https://pmkisan.gov.in) via OTP with Aadhaar-linked mobile or through biometric authentication at any Common Service Center (CSC). Without active e-KYC, subsequent installment disbursements are temporarily suspended.
2. **Land Seeding**: Land records must be verified and seeded with the state revenue database.
3. **Aadhaar-Bank Account Seeding**: The recipient bank account must be actively linked with Aadhaar and NPCI mapping enabled for DBT transfer.

### Troubleshooting Failed Installments / "Payment Stopped":
- Check portal status: Visit https://pmkisan.gov.in > "Know Your Status" > Enter Registration No.
- Common failure codes: "Aadhaar not seeded with NPCI", "Land seeding: NO", "eKYC: Incomplete".
- Rectification: Approach local Village Revenue Officer (Patwari/Talathi) for land record update, or submit DBT Mandate Form at bank branch.
- Official PM-Kisan Helpline: Toll Free **155261** / **011-24300606**.
