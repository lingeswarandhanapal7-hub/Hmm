# Income Tax Notice under Section 148 — Income Escaping Assessment

## Category: Tax & Government Notices
## Citation: Income Tax Act, 1961 — Section 148 & 148A
## Statutory Urgency: Critical (30 days from receipt)

### What Section 148 Means
Section 148 is issued when the Assessing Officer has credible information (such as high-value cash deposits, property purchase, shares transaction reported in SFT/AIS) indicating that taxable income has escaped assessment. Under current law, an inquiry under Section 148A must normally precede Section 148, giving the taxpayer an opportunity of being heard.

### Time Limits:
- Up to 3 years from the end of the relevant assessment year in normal cases.
- Up to 10 years only if the escaped income represented in the form of an asset exceeds ₹50 Lakhs.

### Taxpayer Rights & Immediate Action Steps:
1. **Request Reasons Recorded**: You have a legal right under Supreme Court jurisprudence (GKN Driveshafts precedent) to request a copy of the reasons recorded by the department before submitting detailed arguments.
2. **File Return in Response**: The notice requires you to furnish a fresh return of income within 30 days from service of the notice.
3. **Check Section 148A Order**: Verify whether an order under Section 148A(d) was passed before the issuance of the Section 148 notice.
4. **Consult Qualified Professional**: Do not ignore this notice or attempt informal replies without verifying all bank statements and AIS records with a Chartered Accountant.
