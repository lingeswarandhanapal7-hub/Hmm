# Banking Consumer Protection — Unauthorized Transactions & RBI Liability Rules

## Category: Consumer Rights & Banking Protections
## Citation: RBI Master Circular DBR.No.Leg.BC.78/09.07.005/2017-18
## Statutory Urgency: Critical (Strict 3-day reporting deadline for zero liability)

### RBI Zero Liability Framework
Under the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) circular on Customer Protection – Limiting Liability of Customers in Unauthorized Electronic Banking Transactions:

### Liability Tiers:
1. **Zero Liability (100% Bank Refund)**:
   - Contributory fraud/negligence/deficiency on the part of the bank (whether reported by customer or not).
   - Third-party breach where neither the bank nor the customer is at fault, **provided the customer notifies the bank within 3 working days** of receiving communication regarding the unauthorized transaction.
2. **Limited Liability (₹5,000 to ₹25,000 max)**:
   - If reported between **4 to 7 working days**, the maximum customer liability is capped:
     - Basic savings account (BSBD): ₹5,000 max.
     - Regular savings accounts / credit card limit up to ₹5 Lakh: ₹10,000 max.
     - Current accounts / credit card limit above ₹5 Lakh: ₹25,000 max.
3. **Beyond 7 Working Days**: Liability is determined according to bank board-approved policy.

### Critical Action Steps Within 24-72 Hours:
- **Freeze/Block Immediately**: Call bank emergency helpline to freeze the card/UPI/net banking handle immediately. Note the complaint tracking number and timestamp.
- **National Cybercrime Helpline**: Call **1930** or log complaint on https://cybercrime.gov.in within golden hours to freeze fraudulent money transfers in intermediary accounts.
- **Shadow Credit Mandate**: Under RBI rules, the bank must credit the disputed amount as shadow credit to the customer's account within **10 working days** from the date of notice by the customer.
