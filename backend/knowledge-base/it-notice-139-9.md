# Income Tax Notice under Section 139(9) — Defective Return

## Category: Tax & Government Notices
## Citation: Income Tax Act, 1961 — Section 139(9)
## Statutory Urgency: High (15 days strict deadline)

### What Section 139(9) Means
A notice under Section 139(9) means the Income Tax Department detected an internal inconsistency, missing schedule, or structural error in your submitted ITR. Common triggers include:
- Claiming TDS credit without reporting the corresponding gross income in the P&L or salary schedule.
- Gross turnover or balance sheet not provided when presumptive taxation (Sec 44AD/44ADA) was selected incorrectly.
- Mismatch between taxes paid according to Challan BSR/Challan Serial Number and departmental records.
- Selecting the wrong ITR Form (e.g. filing ITR-1 Sahaj instead of ITR-2 when capital gains or multiple house properties exist).

### Statutory Deadlines & Next Steps:
- **15-Day Correction Window**: The taxpayer has 15 calendar days from receipt of the electronic notice to rectify the defect on the e-filing portal. An extension can be requested before the 15 days expire.
- **Consequences of Ignored Notice**: If no correction is uploaded within 15 days, the return is declared "invalid" (void ab initio). This treats you legally as having never filed your return for that Assessment Year, triggering late filing fees under Section 234F, loss of carry-forward losses, and interest under 234A.
- **Action Required**: Log in to https://eportal.incometax.gov.in > e-File > Response to Defective Notice (Section 139(9)). Choose "Agree" and upload the corrected return file or select "Disagree" with explanatory justification.
